    Install Legal Hospital on Mobile

    No Play Store or App Store fee required. Install directly as a native-feel mobile app on both Android and iPhone.

    🤖

    For Android (Chrome Browser)

    1. Open your GitHub Pages link in Google Chrome.
    2. Tap the Three Dots (⋮) menu in the top-right corner.
    3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
    4. Tap Install — Legal Hospital will appear in your app drawer!
    🍎

    For iPhone / iPad (Safari Browser)

    1. Open your GitHub Pages link in Apple Safari.
    2. Tap the Share icon (□↑) at the bottom center of the screen.
    3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
    4. Tap Add — Launches full-screen just like an App Store app!
